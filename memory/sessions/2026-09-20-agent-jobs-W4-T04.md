## Handoff

**Next action:** `W4-T07` — the job feed for providers. `POST /api/jobs/:id/quotes` exists and
nothing lists the jobs worth quoting on, so every quote in the system today was written by somebody
handed a uuid. `W3-T05`'s `ST_DWithin` query is the same shape with the operands swapped.

**Two things `W4-T04` learned that `W4-T07` needs on day one:**

1. **Page it from the start.** The rule that came out of this ticket is *a list written by other
   people is the one that must be bounded* — a job feed is exactly that, more so than the quote
   list was.
2. **The ranking a feed wants probably cannot be its paging order.** `ratingAvg` could not be a
   sort key because `CursorValue` cannot carry a null; "best match" will hit the same wall. Decide
   early whether the ranking is a database sort or a presentation order over a loaded set.

**Do not redo:**
- The index predicate. `WHERE status IN ('PENDING','ACCEPTED')` is three decisions, two of them the
  operator's, all three argued in spec §2.4 (`MEM-2026-09-20-30`).
- Accepting leaving the siblings `PENDING` and the job `OPEN`. That is ADR-013 §4, not an omission.
- `ACCEPTED` being terminal with no un-accept. It is a declared debt with the rule for paying it
  written into `quoteMachine`; `W4-T05` takes it out of `terminal` in the change that gives it an
  exit.
- The absence of an axe pass on the two screens. No harness here can reach a page behind a session;
  it is named in spec §6 with `agent-ui` as the owner.

**What a reviewer should push back on** (also in the run record): the audit-write-before-update
ordering in `decide()`, the response-shape change on `GET /api/jobs/:id/quotes` without an ADR, and
whether *"a rejection frees the slot"* was read correctly — nothing fails if it was not, which is
what makes it worth checking.
